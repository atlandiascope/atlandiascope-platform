Platform approach

A consistent experience, with security at the core.

The intended architecture connects separate product services through verified identity, server-side permissions, and auditable workflows.

One customer identity

Supabase Auth provides account sign-in. Unified identity and single sign-on across connected product services remain in progress.

Product-level access

Each service will enforce its own entitlements and API scopes on the server.

Human-controlled actions

Diagnostics may recommend actions; consequential repairs remain subject to explicit approval.
